SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: May 2026

Program Name

Urban PL — Competitive Pickup Soccer Platform

Program Description

Urban PL sends SMS notifications to registered players who have opted in via the Urban PL mobile app. Messages include game confirmations, team assignments, lineup notifications, match reminders, and match results.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your game schedule. You may receive up to 5 messages per game you are registered for (confirmation, team assignment, reminders, and result). You will not receive messages for games you have not joined.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard SMS and data rates charged by your mobile carrier apply to all messages sent by Urban PL. Urban PL does not charge separately for SMS messages.

How to Opt In

Users opt in by downloading the Urban PL app or visiting the Urban PL website at theurbanpl.com/signup.html, creating an account, entering their mobile phone number, and checking the optional SMS consent checkbox during signup. The checkbox reads:

"Optional: I agree to receive SMS notifications from Urban PL, including game confirmations, team assignments, lineup notifications, and match reminders. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help."

SMS consent is completely optional. You may create an account and use all Urban PL features without opting in to SMS notifications. Consent is not required as a condition of service. When provided, consent is stored securely in the Urban PL database with the player record.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any Urban PL SMS message. You will receive a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To re-subscribe, reply START.

You may also withdraw consent within the Urban PL app under Profile > Settings.

How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any Urban PL SMS message for assistance, or contact us directly:

📧 admin@theurbanpl.com

Supported Carriers

Urban PL SMS notifications are supported by all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carrier support may vary.

Privacy

Your phone number and consent status are stored securely and are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
