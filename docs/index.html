Features

Everything a league needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Built for players who are serious about their game, without the serious overhead.

⚖️

Smart Team Balancing

Teams are automatically balanced by player rating 2 hours before kickoff. Fair games, every time.

🏆

Cups & Tournaments

Enter team tournaments, compete for the cup, and take bragging rights back to your city.

📍

Real Maps & Directions

Every game shows the exact location on a map. One tap gets you directions in Apple or Google Maps.

💬

SMS Notifications

Get texts for team assignments, game reminders, and match results. Never miss a kickoff or wonder what team you're on.

📈

City Rankings

A live leaderboard ranks every player by points, goals, wins, and games played. Your reputation follows you everywhere.

🔔

Game Reminders

Push notifications remind you before kickoff so you never miss a game you signed up for.

💳

Secure Payments

Pay entry fees securely through Stripe. Free games join instantly. Paid games are one tap.

🌎

English & Spanish

The app speaks your language. Switch between English and Spanish in one tap from your profile.

🎬

Highlights — Coming Soon

AI-powered personal highlight clips from every game. Your best moments, automatically cut and ready to share.

For Referees

Run the game.
Get paid.

Urban PL has a dedicated referee platform. Manage fixtures, run live matches, and earn per game.

  • Dedicated Referee App
    A full match management panel — attendance, live timer, goals, cards — all from your phone.
  • 💰
    Earn Per Game
    Get paid for every match you officiate. Bonus earned after 5 completed games.
  • 📅
    Set Your Availability
    Mark when you're free and the admin assigns you to games that fit your schedule.
  • ✅
    Verified Identity
    All referees go through ID verification. Players know they're getting a real official.

Become a Referee

Apply through the app. Submit your credentials and ID. Start officiating once approved.
